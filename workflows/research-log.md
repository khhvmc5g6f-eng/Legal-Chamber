# Research Log (template)

Copy this table into a matter's `research/` directory and append a row per search actually run. This is what makes "a disconfirming search was run" checkable rather than asserted - see `../docs/QUALITY_GATES.md` Gates 4 and 6.

## Rejected authority log

A separate note on why an apparently relevant authority was not relied upon - useful on later review, and required if a reviewer later asks "did you consider X."
