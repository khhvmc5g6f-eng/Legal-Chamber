## Case-specific findings from the seven matters (not repository defects - these are the adversarial/counsel/quality-review process correctly finding gaps in intentionally sparse fictional scenarios)

Across the six matters that completed on the first pass, agents raised 144 findings in total (M1: 29, M2: 17, M3: 22, M5: 29, M6: 27, M7: 20). The large majority are exactly what a working counsel-review/quality-check process should produce against a deliberately underspecified fictional scenario: missing dates, unestablished disability status, unparticularised grounds, missing PCP identification, unproven service-level breach quantum, and similar. These are evidence the adversarial review layer works (see `LEGAL_CHAMBERS_STRESS_TEST_REPORT.md`'s Opposition Quality and Judicial Neutrality sections), not defects in the repository - they are not itemised individually here. The register above extracts only the findings that pointed at something wrong with the repository itself, reproducible independent of which fictional facts were fed in.
