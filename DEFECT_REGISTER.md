## Case-specific findings from the seven matters (not repository defects - these are the adversarial/counsel/quality-review process correctly finding gaps in intentionally sparse fictional scenarios)

Across the six matters that completed on the first pass, agents raised 144 findings in total (M1: 29, M2: 17, M3: 22, M5: 29, M6: 27, M7: 20). The large majority are exactly what a working counsel-review/quality-check process should produce against a deliberately underspecified fictional scenario: missing dates, unestablished disability status, unparticularised grounds, missing PCP identification, unproven service-level breach quantum, and similar. These are evidence the adversarial review layer works (see `LEGAL_CHAMBER_STRESS_TEST_REPORT.md`'s Opposition Quality and Judicial Neutrality sections), not defects in the repository - they are not itemised individually here. The register above extracts only the findings that pointed at something wrong with the repository itself, reproducible independent of which fictional facts were fed in.

## Known gaps not addressed in this pass (tracked, not silently dropped)

- No automated mechanism enforces D-03/D-05's new instructions - they are prompt-level rules, checked by the quality agent's own diligence, not a code-level gate. Consistent with `docs/HONEST_STATUS.md`'s existing statement that this is a prompt-based system with no execution sandbox.
- `regulators/` remains empty (CNIL and others) - M5 correctly flagged this as a known, already-documented gap, not new.
- The EU pack (D-02) is `STRUCTURAL_DRAFT` like the other 8 packs - no populated CJEU case-law database.
