# Consejo General de Enfermería (CGE) and the provincial Colegios de Enfermería

A regulator profile in this repository, following `ico.md`'s format and mirroring `cgcom.md`'s structure for the medical profession (see `README.md`). Nursing in Spain has the same two-tier national/regional split as medicine — a national Consejo General plus provincial Colegios, and Autonomous Community Councils in between — and it is easy to conflate the national body's coordinating role with the provincial Colegio's actual licensing/disciplinary role, exactly as with CGCOM.

## Statutory basis

The Consejo General de Enfermería (also referred to in older material as the Consejo General de Colegios Oficiales de Enfermería, part of the "Organización Colegial de Enfermería de España") is governed by Estatutos generales approved by **Real Decreto 1231/2001**, of 8 November ("Estatutos generales de la Organización Colegial de Enfermería de España, del Consejo General y de Ordenación de la actividad profesional de enfermería"). This title and the decree's existence were confirmed via a BOE search-result listing this session; as with `cgcom.md`, the decree's full article text could not be directly fetched — see "What is NOT in this profile".

## Structure: CGE vs the provincial Colegios

Per a direct fetch of `consejogeneralenfermeria.org`'s institutional pages (checked 2026-08-22), the CGE describes itself as the entity that "groups, coordinates and represents with exclusive character the nursing profession" at national and international level, and states it "orders... the professional exercise" and "exercises disciplinary faculty and resolves appeals that are filed, in the terms regulated in its Statutes." The organisation's own structure page describes a multi-tiered framework:

- A national executive body and plenary assembly (the Consejo General itself)
- **Autonomous Community Councils**, coordinating regional nursing affairs
- **Provincial Colegios** — the local nursing colleges

The site's own framing places the provincial and regional bodies as operating "under the CGE's broader authority" — but, as with CGCOM, "broader authority" describes coordination and appellate review, not that the CGE itself performs an individual nurse's registration. The CGE also runs a **National Deontological Commission** responsible for ethical standards and disciplinary matters at national level, alongside a "good conduct certificate" service.

**Colegiación (mandatory professional registration)** is, per a WebSearch-sourced finding from a `consejogeneralenfermeria.org` news item on a Tribunal Constitucional ruling (checked 2026-08-22, headline: "El Tribunal Constitucional respalda la colegiación obligatoria de los profesionales sanitarios"), confirmed as a real, litigated, and upheld requirement for practising in a regulated health profession in Spain, and — per the same search pass — it is "sufficient to be incorporated into [one's] professional college to practise throughout the entire territory of the State" once registered with the Colegio for one's territorial area. A secondary-source note surfaced in the same search (not independently verified against a primary text this session) suggested the *mandatory* character of registration can currently depend on the Autonomous Community pending further national-level legal development — this is flagged here as an unverified nuance, not asserted as fact.

Provincial Colegios hold **first-instance sanctioning competence** over their own registered members for professional/collegiate infractions, per a WebSearch finding (checked 2026-08-22) summarising general Spanish professional-college law: "Professional colleges have competence to sanction members who incur infractions in the professional and collegiate order," and any such sanctioning power must follow the same due-process principles (presumption of innocence, right to be heard) that govern Spanish public-administration sanctioning procedures generally.

No specific regional Colegio de Enfermería (e.g. Madrid's CODEM) was successfully fetched and confirmed live this session — an attempt timed out — so, unlike `cgcom.md`'s ICOMEM example, this profile does not carry an independently verified regional worked example for nursing. That is a known gap, not a claim that no such example exists.

## Appeal route and forum

Confirmed this session: the CGE "resolves appeals... in the terms regulated in its Statutes" (per `consejogeneralenfermeria.org`), indicating the same internal-appeal pattern as CGCOM — a nurse can appeal a provincial Colegio decision up to the national Consejo General. As with `cgcom.md`, the further judicial route beyond the Consejo General (presumably the contentious-administrative jurisdiction, given Colegios' public-law-corporation status) was **not** independently confirmed live this session and is flagged `UNVERIFIED` rather than asserted.
