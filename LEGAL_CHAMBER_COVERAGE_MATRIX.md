## Reading this matrix honestly

"PASS" here means: real evidence from an actual execution supports the capability working as designed for the matters that exercised it. It does not mean "will always work" - six matters is a real but small sample, and the single most important qualifier across the whole matrix is D-03 (claimed-but-not-persisted outputs), which shows that even within a single successful-looking run, a stage's own narrative can outrun what it actually did. That defect is fixed at the instruction level (agents/quality/ROLE.md), not at a level that makes it structurally impossible to recur - see `docs/HONEST_STATUS.md`.
