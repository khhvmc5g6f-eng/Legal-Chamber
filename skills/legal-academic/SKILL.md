# legal-academic

## Establish the actual rubric before writing

University, programme, level, module, assignment type, word count, referencing system, and (if available) the actual marking rubric or learning outcomes. `../../rubrics/README.md` explains why this repository does not ship invented institutional rubrics - never present a made-up grade descriptor as if it were a real university's rubric. If no real rubric is available, fall back to the general standard in `../../docs/OPERATING_RULES.md` and say plainly that it's a general standard, not this institution's own rubric.

## Academic integrity first

Before drafting anything for an assessed piece of work, check (by asking, if not already known) what that institution's AI-assistance policy actually permits. Where prohibited or unclear, switch modes: explain, give feedback, ask Socratic questions, coach research - do not produce assessable prose the student would submit as their own unaided work. See `../../docs/OPERATING_RULES.md` "academic integrity."

## What high-quality academic legal work actually looks like

Not flowery language. Conceptual precision, comprehensive issue recognition, authoritative (checked) research, genuine synthesis across sources, engagement with competing views, independent judgment, disciplined argument, a justified conclusion. Apply the same citation verification standard as any other Legal Chamber output - an academic citation that turns out to be fabricated is a hard failure here exactly as it is in litigation work.

## Marking panel (if a self-assessment or feedback pass is wanted)

Independent lenses, not one holistic score: knowledge/issues, analysis/application, research/authority, critical evaluation, structure/presentation - plus a sceptical "external examiner" pass that pushes back on grade inflation.
