## Paragraph-by-paragraph content rule

Every paragraph must be something this specific witness actually knows from personal knowledge, or must say plainly where the information came from if not personal knowledge (a document, something they were told, and by whom). Never insert a fact the witness has not actually supplied, even if it would help the case - see `../skills/legal-evidence/SKILL.md`.
