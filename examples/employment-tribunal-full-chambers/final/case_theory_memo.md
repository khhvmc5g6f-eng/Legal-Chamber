## 3. The issue tree and the lead theory

Full element-by-element detail already exists at `issues/issues.json` (ISS-001 to ISS-009) and is not restated wholesale here. The theory:

**Lead heads: ISS-003 (automatic unfair dismissal, ERA 1996 s.103A) and ISS-004 (whistleblowing detriment, ERA 1996 s.47B).** Chosen as the lead theory for three independent reasons, not one: (a) no qualifying period of continuous employment is required, unlike ISS-002, so F-008's unknown length of service cannot defeat these heads even if it turns out to be under two years; (b) the remedy is materially stronger in principle (see Section 2); (c) real, verified, on-point binding authority supports the doctrine:

- ***Kuzel v Roche Products Ltd*** [2008] EWCA Civ 380 (AUTH-003, `VERIFIED_SECONDARY_SOURCE`, `BINDING`, treatment `FOLLOWED`) - the legal burden of showing the reason for dismissal never shifts to the claimant; once the claimant produces some evidence the protected disclosure was the reason, the Respondent must show its own stated reason, and if that is rejected the Tribunal must consider whether the claimant's alleged reason was the true one.
- ***Beatt v Croydon Health Services NHS Trust*** [2017] EWCA Civ 401 (AUTH-004, `VERIFIED_SECONDARY_SOURCE`, `BINDING`, `FOLLOWED`) - whether a disclosure was protected is for the Tribunal to decide objectively, not a question of the employer's own belief. Itself a genuine NHS whistleblowing dismissal case, correctly named and citable, in direct and deliberate contrast to the fabricated authority addressed in Section 5.
- ***NHS Manchester v Fecitt and others*** [2011] EWCA Civ 1190 (AUTH-002, `VERIFIED_SECONDARY_SOURCE`, `BINDING`, `FOLLOWED` as to causation) - the s.47B causation threshold is "materially influenced," not the stricter EU-discrimination "in no sense whatsoever" test. **Applied here with a correction this memo makes for the first time in this matter's record**: Fecitt's separate holding that an employer bears no vicarious liability for a co-worker's detriment was superseded with effect from 25 June 2013 by ERA 1996 s.47B(1A)-(1E) (inserted by Enterprise and Regulatory Reform Act 2013 s.19), independently fetched and confirmed this session at `legislation.gov.uk` (`authorities.json` AUTH-002, now updated `VERIFIED_PRIMARY_SOURCE` for this specific qualification). This matters concretely on these facts: if the "contradictory internal emails" (F-013) or aspects of the disciplinary process (F-011) were the acts of a co-worker rather than a formal management decision, s.47B(1A) gives the Claimant a direct route against that co-worker and treats it as also done by the Respondent - a materially stronger position than Fecitt's own 2011 holding would allow, and one three prior moot hearings in this matter's own record flagged as an outstanding, unaddressed repair without ever actually applying it. It is applied here, for the first time, with a primary source behind it.

**Authority-weight discipline applied per `agents/solicitors/ROLE.md`**: all four authorities above carry `authority_weight: BINDING` in `authorities.json` (Court of Appeal decisions binding on the Employment Tribunal per the hierarchy in `jurisdictions/england-wales/README.md`) and are treated as such, not as merely persuasive or illustrative. None is currently `authority_weight: SECONDARY` masquerading as decisive - the specific failure mode `agents/solicitors/ROLE.md` names as having been caught in a prior stress test is checked and does not recur here.

**What the lead theory needs and does not yet have**: exactly what `chronology_gaps.md` and `issues.json` already say - the content, recipient, form, and date of the protected disclosure(s) (F-010), and their sequencing against the disciplinary process (F-012). Kuzel and Beatt both require something concrete to test; a bare assertion that "protected disclosures were made" states a cause of action, it does not prove one. This is disclosed as a real, current weakness, not glossed over - consistent with Hearing 3's finding that no Evidence Ledger exists for this matter at all.

**Secondary head: ISS-002 (ordinary unfair dismissal).** Runs only as a contingent fallback pending confirmation that continuity of employment (F-008) clears the ERA 1996 s.108 two-year threshold. If it does not, this head fails for want of jurisdiction outright, independent of the merits - named plainly rather than hoped past.

**Parasitic issue: ISS-009 (procedural unfairness)**, relevant to remedy (Polkey; ACAS Code uplift) only once a liability head succeeds, not a free-standing claim.

## 4. The disability-discrimination cluster and victimisation: not presently pleadable, and this memo does not pretend otherwise

`agents/solicitors/ROLE.md`'s non-negotiable requires naming an unfavourable conclusion, not omitting it because it weakens the overall theory. On the facts as supplied:

- **ISS-005 (disability status, threshold gateway for ISS-006/ISS-007)**: F-021 confirms no impairment is named at all - not a diagnosis, not even a lay description. A disability discrimination claim cannot be pleaded, let alone proved, without identifying the impairment relied on. This is a pleading defect, not a thin evidence base, and this matter's own moot record (Hearing 1) correctly struck it on that basis.
- **ISS-007 (reasonable adjustments)**: F-024 confirms no PCP and no adjustment sought or refused is identified anywhere. ***Environment Agency v Rowan*** [2008] ICR 218 (AUTH-006, `VERIFIED_SECONDARY_SOURCE`, `PERSUASIVE`, `FOLLOWED`) holds a reasonable-adjustments claim requires the PCP, the comparator, and the disadvantage to be identified before reasonableness can even be assessed - this is not a drafting nicety this theory can work around, it is the claim itself.
- **ISS-006 (discrimination arising from disability)**: derivatively fails while ISS-005 is unpleaded - "something arising in consequence of disability" cannot be assessed while the disability itself is unidentified.
- **ISS-008 (victimisation)**: F-023 confirms no specific protected act under Equality Act 2010 s.27(2) is identified.

**This memo's position, consistent with Hearing 1's disposition and not disturbed by anything since**: these four heads should not be carried forward in their current form. The sustainable course is to obtain the actual OH material, sickness absence records, and a specific account of what adjustment was sought and refused (or what protected act was done) from the client directly, and only then decide whether to plead ISS-005/006/007/008 with real content, or drop them. If OH material or sickness records, once actually read, do identify a impairment and its effects, ***Gallop v Newport City Council*** [2013] EWCA Civ 1583 (AUTH-005, `VERIFIED_SECONDARY_SOURCE`, `BINDING`, `FOLLOWED`) is directly available and should be pleaded then: an employer cannot simply rubber-stamp an OH adviser's view on disability status or knowledge, it must exercise its own judgement - relevant to the s.15(2)/knowledge element of ISS-006 and the knowledge element of ISS-007 alike, once there is an actual disability to attach it to.
